About Project

Exploring Human Flourishing

If AI dramatically increases humanity's productive capability, how could we use that capability to ensure that every person has reliable access to basic food, shelter, energy, and knowledge—reducing survival pressure and giving people greater freedom to pursue lives they genuinely care about?

A Tool for Possibility, Not Idealism

This application is not intended to claim that artificial intelligence will automatically solve poverty, eliminate scarcity, or guarantee universal prosperity. Technology alone does not determine social outcomes.

Real-world implementation depends on economics, physical infrastructure, institutions, policy, human governance, and cultural alignment. Therefore, every serious idea in this deck includes a Reality Check detailing potential pitfalls, resource requirements, and unintended consequences.

How to Interact

• Swipe Right / Click Save: Store an idea in your session library.
• Swipe Left / Click Pass: Move past the current idea.
• Swipe Up / Click Explore: Dive into implementation, risks, and feasibility models.
• Filter Bar: Narrow down ideas by focus areas like Food, Shelter, Abundance, or Robotics.
